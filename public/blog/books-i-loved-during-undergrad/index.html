Books I Loved During These 4 Years in Undergrad

2 min

On the Road — Possibly a book that has the biggest impact on me. Possibly puts a mirror in front of you of what it means to be alive and free.

After Dark — One of Murakami’s most trippy and surrealist works. Literally requires a different state of mind to digest.

Masters of Doom — The story of the two guys who created the most iconic software in programming history. Highly recommended to both programmers and designers.

City of Thieves — Found this in a reading slump. Finished it in two days. Amazing characters, beautiful narrative.

Dark Matter — That sci-fi series on Apple. I felt really bad for the protagonist.. God I hope no one goes through this.

2001: A Space Odyssey — My first Arthur Clarke. Amazing start, the ending left me quite tripped out.

The Town and the City — Not a book to be completed, but savoured. Kerouac’s most criminally underrated work.

Post Office — My first Bukowski. Hooked from the first para. Read it just to know what it means to be a master of the craft.

A Long Way to a Small Angry Planet — A sci-fi that actually emphasises characters. Great ideas around identity, friendship and love.

Fundamentals of Computer Organization and Architecture — One of the most beautiful books I read as a student. Shows you how a computer really works.

Hackers: Heroes of the Computer Revolution — Recommended to LITERALLY any programmer. Makes you appreciate everyone who came before us.
